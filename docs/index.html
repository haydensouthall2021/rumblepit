How a round works

  1. Launch

    A coin launches on Raydium LaunchLab and trades from its first second, so it shows up on DexScreener and every wallet straight away.

  2. Join a league

    At launch the coin picks one of six leagues, for good. Up to 128 coins share a league and its pot.

  3. Burn to back it

    Holders burn the coin to score for it. Burned tokens are destroyed forever, which also cuts the supply.

  4. The bell

    After six hours the league's rules pick one winning coin. Its single biggest burner that round gets the entire pot.

Where the money comes from

Every trade on a Rumble Pit coin pays 1.5%. 0.5% goes to the coin's creator. 1% goes to its league, where the program splits it half to the pot, half to the platform. That split is enforced on-chain.

Launching is free. When a coin graduates, 100% of its liquidity is burned, so nobody can pull it.

Anyone can ring the bell once a round is over, and the program alone decides who is paid. Pausing the platform can't stop a payout.
