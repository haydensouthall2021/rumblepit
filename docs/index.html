How it works

No sign-ups and no claiming. Every step is a Solana transaction you can check.

  1. Launch

    Launch a coin from this page. It goes live on Raydium LaunchLab, so it trades and shows on charts from the first buy.

  2. Join a league

    At launch the coin picks one league for life. A league holds up to 128 coins in pools of 16, all sharing one pot.

  3. Burn to score

    Holders burn their coin to score for it. Burned tokens are destroyed for good. Each burn is at least 0.10% of that coin's supply.

  4. The bell

    Every hour the round ends. Anyone can ring the bell; the program picks the winner and pays the pot in the same transaction.

Rumble Pit's 1% is routed into the coin's own league. The program then sends 50% to that league's pot and the rest to the treasury. The split is enforced on-chain.

After graduation the coin trades in a standard Raydium pool: 0.25% to Raydium and 0.05% to the creator. Rumble Pit takes nothing after graduation, so a coin feeds its league's pot while it's on the curve.

Launching a coin is free. Joining a league costs nothing.
